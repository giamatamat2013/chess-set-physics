## Description

Two sides, two kinds of physics. White plays classical physics, black plays quantum physics. Each piece type has the same height and outline on both sides, so the board still reads like normal chess.

**White: classical physics**
- **King: gravity well.** A funnel of curved space-time, with the grid cut into it and a planet sitting in its mouth.
- **Queen: electromagnetic wave.** The electric and magnetic fields as two sine-shaped fins at right angles, with a crown of photons.
- **Bishop: prism.** A ray of white light goes in on one side and leaves as a fan of colours.
- **Knight: magnet.** A horse with a horseshoe magnet for an ear and a mane shaped like a wave.
- **Rook: Tesla coil.** A primary winding, a tall secondary coil and a notched top-load torus.
- **Pawn: atom.** A nucleus with three electron orbits.

**Black: quantum physics**
- **King: Bloch sphere.** The qubit's sphere, with the equator, two meridians and the state arrow. The z axis rises out of it as the cross.
- **Queen: double slit.** A plane wave climbs to a wall with two slits. Above it the interference pattern stands up like a crown.
- **Bishop: Schrodinger's box.** A closed box marked with psi, with the cat's ears poking out under the lid.
- **Knight: superposition.** The front of the head is smooth and the back half is drawn as waves. The mane is quantised, a row of beads.
- **Rook: quantum tunnelling.** A wave runs into a barrier, dies down inside it and comes out the far side smaller.
- **Pawn: p orbital.** Two lobes meet at the nucleus, marked with the + and - phase of the wave function.

All pieces stand on a round base. White bases have dial ticks; black bases have energy-level grooves.

**Printing**
- Every piece is one solid part, designed with overhangs of about 45 degrees so it prints without supports. Each file was checked slice by slice for mid-air starts, thin necks and thin walls.
- Print the classical pieces in a light colour and the quantum pieces in a dark one.
- A whole side (16 pieces) fits on one plate, 166 x 162 mm.

**Files**
- `classical_king_gravity_well`, `classical_queen_em_wave`: 1 each
- `classical_bishop_prism`, `classical_knight_magnet`, `classical_rook_tesla_coil`: 2 each
